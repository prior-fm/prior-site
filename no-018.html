Why these pages

The page is part of the lesson.

Every source in the films is shown as the actual page, captured on 2026-09-04 at phone width and scrolled the way you would scroll it, in the page’s own colours rather than Prior’s. That is deliberate: the point is that you open one.

Every source is free to learn in full. The films say “free” and never “certified”: no provider’s own page confirmed to us that a certificate costs nothing, and the ones that do offer one either charge for it or were not confirmed. The roadmap says which is which.
