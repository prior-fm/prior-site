How it’s made

An AI agent draws every frame in code.

There is no drawing tablet here. Every line in a Butter episode, from the cross-hatching on the ant to the grain in the table, is written as code by an AI agent, then rendered into a loop. It makes the same three passes an illustrator would, and it is a small demonstration of what the episodes explain: give an agent clear context and tight boundaries, and it does careful work.

  1. 01Blue guidesConstruction circles and dashed lines block out the shapes.
  2. 02InkContours go down over the guides, with a slight hand-drawn wobble.
  3. 03Hatch & colourEngraved hatching for shade, flat paper colours, one gold “important thing”.
