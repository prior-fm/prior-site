Sixteen ideas in machine learning. One free source for each, in the order the films teach them.

The films count a month of screen time as 240 squares, one an hour. Put the same hours somewhere else: one hour a day is 30 hours and the intuition. Eight hours a day is 240, and you can read how ChatGPT works from the inside. Pick the hours you have. The blocks add up, the way the films count them.

Free, and what that means

Every source on this page is free to learn in full, checked against the provider’s own page on 2026-09-04 where it could be read, and against second-hand listings where it could not. Certificates are a separate question. Coursera’s is paid; the audit is free. Kaggle Learn describes a certificate on completion, and we have not confirmed from Kaggle’s own page that it costs nothing. If a certificate is the point for you, read the provider’s page before you plan on it. None of these is needed to learn the sixteen.

Hours marked with a provider’s name are that provider’s figure. “About” is ours.
