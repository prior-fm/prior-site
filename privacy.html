What we
collect

Analytics. Page counts via GoatCounter. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal data, no advertising identifiers. It records the page, the referrer and a rough country.

Email. If you subscribe, your address is held by Buttondown and used only to send the weekly issue. It is never sold or shared. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.

Video. Videos are served from this site directly. No embedded player, so no third-party tracking from watching them.

Hosting. The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Questions, or want your address removed? Reply to any issue.
