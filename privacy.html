Privacy
What we
collect

Analytics. Page counts via GoatCounter. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no personal data, no advertising identifiers. It records the page, the referrer and a rough country.

Email. If you subscribe, your address is held by Buttondown and used only to send the weekly issue. It is never sold or shared. Every email carries a one-click unsubscribe.

Video. Videos are served from this site directly. No embedded player, so no third-party tracking from watching them.

Hosting. The site is static and hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Instagram messaging. If you comment a keyword on one of our Instagram posts, our app sends you the link to that issue’s page as a private reply. To do that we store your Instagram-scoped sender ID and the keyword you asked for, so your reply can be matched to your request — it is deleted the moment the link is delivered. We also keep the ID of any comment already answered, for 30 days, so nobody is messaged twice. We store no names, no profile data, no comment text and no message content. Nothing is sold, shared, or passed to any third party; the only service the app talks to is Meta’s own API.

Deleting your data. Message @prior.fm on Instagram with the word DELETE, or reply to any issue. Everything tied to your account is removed within 7 days.

Questions, or want your address removed? Reply to any issue.
