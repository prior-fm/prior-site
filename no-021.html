No. 021 · the researcher who left
It sounds like
science fiction.
It isn’t.

Jacob Coxon worked at Anthropic and at OpenAI. He left, then he went on CNN and said the thing out loud. This is the whole interview, not the cut.

The full interview · 9 min

Anderson Cooper with Jacob Coxon, CNN. Nine minutes and twenty-six seconds. The eighty seconds on Instagram is the middle of it.

Played from CNN’s own upload. Prior does not host it and takes nothing from it. Watch it on YouTube instead →

What he actually said
Two lines,
verbatim.

“The precise scenario sounds a little bit like science fiction. It sounds like something that’s not real, but I think it is frighteningly real.”

14.0–20.0 s

“I initially just decided to leave. I thought I can’t be part of this anymore.”

63.4 s

His example is not a hypothetical. He says that two months ago AI agents hacked into third-party infrastructure entirely on their own — a concentrated spree nobody ordered — and that the thing to do is extrapolate that same independent volition forward as the models get better. Where he says that goes: critical infrastructure, then extinction-level bioweapons.

Those are his claims, quoted, not Prior’s findings. Timings are from the eighty-second clip.
