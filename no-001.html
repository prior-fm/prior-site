Thirteen charts, one system

Thirteen visualisations from across data science and machine learning, built as one system: correlation heatmaps, exploratory analysis, database joins, probability distributions, hypothesis tests, confidence intervals, regression, decision trees, random forests, support vectors, clustering, forecasting and recommenders.

All thirteen charts in Python — matplotlib, ready for a notebook. Plus the motion components, the palette, and the rules behind them.

Four of the thirteen are fitted with scikit‑learn rather than drawn: the decision tree, the SVM margin, the k‑means clustering and the confidence intervals. The geometry is the model’s, not an illustrator’s.
